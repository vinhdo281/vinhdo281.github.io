PART 1 Problem Formulation: Why Choose Fuzzy for Inverted Pendulum? TEIL 1 Problemstellung: Warum Fuzzy für das inverse Pendel?

The inverted pendulum on a cart system (Inverted Pendulum on a Cart) is a benchmark problem in Automatic Control & Mechatronics. It represents a classic Underactuated System:

Das inverse Pendel auf einem Wagen (Inverted Pendulum on a Cart) ist ein Benchmark-Problem in der Regelungstechnik und Mechatronik. Es ist ein typisches unteraktuiertes System (Underactuated System):

  • 2 Degrees of Freedom (2-DOF): Cart translational position $x(t)$ and pendulum tilt angle $ heta(t)$.
  • Single Control Input: Horizontal force $F(t)$ applied directly to the cart body. The pendulum rotational joint is completely passive (no actuator).
  • 2 Freiheitsgrade (2-DOF): Translationsposition des Wagens $x(t)$ und Auslenkungswinkel des Pendels $ heta(t)$.
  • Einziger Steuereingang: Horizontale Stellkraft $F(t)$ direkt auf den Wagen. Das Drehgelenk des Pendels ist völlig passiv (kein Motor).
⚠️
The Pitfall of Linear Controllers (PID / LQR):
When designing PID or LQR, dynamic equations must be linearized around the upright equilibrium point $ heta pprox 0$ based on small-angle assumptions: $\sin heta pprox heta$, $\cos heta pprox 1$, and $\dot{ heta}^2 pprox 0$.
However, in practice when the initial tilt angle is large ($ heta_0 \ge 15^\circ - 20^\circ$) or under strong external disturbances, nonlinear terms explode, the fixed Jacobian matrix loses validity, pushing the pendulum outside its basin of attraction and causing catastrophic collapse!
Die Falle linearer Regler (PID / LQR):
Beim Entwurf von PID oder LQR müssen die Bewegungsgleichungen um die aufrechte Ruhelage $ heta pprox 0$ unter der Kleinwinkelnäherung linearisiert werden: $\sin heta pprox heta$, $\cos heta pprox 1$ und $\dot{ heta}^2 pprox 0$.
In der Praxis jedoch, bei großen Anfangswinkeln ($ heta_0 \ge 15^\circ - 20^\circ$) oder starken Stoßeinwirkungen, dominieren Nichtlinearitäten, die konstante Jacobi-Matrix verliert ihre Gültigkeit und das Pendel kippt unweigerlich um!

The Fuzzy Logic Controller (Mamdani FIS) overcomes this barrier by mimicking human intuitive heuristic reflexes:

Der Fuzzy-Logic-Controller (Mamdani-FIS) überwindet diese Hürde, indem er die intuitive menschliche Reflexregelung nachbildet:

  • No need to solve complex nonlinear differential equations or rely on local linearization.
  • Automatically ramps up maximal corrective force at critical tilts, while smoothly throttling down when the pendulum returns under momentum.
  • Intelligently coordinates all 4 state variables $( heta, \dot{ heta}, x, \dot{x})$ to stabilize the pendulum upright while returning the cart precisely to center (Zero Cart Drift).
  • Keine Notwendigkeit zur Lösung komplexer nichtlinearer Differentialgleichungen oder lokalen Linearisierung.
  • Regelt in kritischen Kipplagen automatisch mit maximaler Stellkraft gegen und drosselt sanft ab, sobald das Pendel zurückschwingt.
  • Kombiniert geschickt alle 4 Zustandsgrößen $( heta, \dot{ heta}, x, \dot{x})$ zur perfekten Aufrichtung und Rückführung des Wagens zur Schienenmitte (Zero Cart Drift).

PART 4 Designing 16-Rule Fuzzy System with MATLAB (Based on Fuzzy_moi.mlx) TEIL 4 Entwurf des 16-Regeln-Fuzzy-Systems mit MATLAB (Basierend auf Fuzzy_moi.mlx)

Based on Fuzzy_moi.mlx, we formulate a Single Unified Mamdani FIS architecture featuring 4 state inputs and 1 control force output.

Basierend auf Fuzzy_moi.mlx entwickeln wir eine vereinheitlichte Mamdani-FIS-Architektur mit 4 Zustandseingängen und 1 Stellkraftausgang.

4.1 Configuration of 4 Input State Variables 4.1 Konfiguration der 4 Zustandseingänge

Each input is fuzzified into 2 linguistic sets: N (Negative) and P (Positive). To yield an ultra-smooth control surface free of chattering or discontinuities, Z-shaped (zmf) and S-shaped (smf) spline membership functions are used:

Jeder Eingang wird in 2 linguistische Terme unterteilt: N (Negativ) und P (Positiv). Für eine ruckfreie, stetig differenzierbare Stellfläche kommen Z-förmige (zmf) und S-förmige (smf) Zugehörigkeitsfunktionen zum Einsatz:

4.2 Output Configuration (Control Force $F$) 4.2 Ausgangskonfiguration (Stellkraft $F$)

The output control force $F$ operates within $[-12, +12]\ ext{N}$, defined using 4 generalized bell-shaped functions (`gbellmf`):

Die Ausgangsstellkraft $F$ liegt im Bereich $[-12, +12]\ ext{N}$ und wird durch 4 Glockenkurven (`gbellmf`) moduliert:

  • NL (Negative Large): Center $c = -10 ext{ N}$, width $a = 3.0 ext{ N}$, slope $b = 2$.
  • NM (Negative Medium): Center $c = -7 ext{ N}$, width $a = 2.5 ext{ N}$, slope $b = 2$.
  • PM (Positive Medium): Center $c = +7 ext{ N}$, width $a = 2.5 ext{ N}$, slope $b = 2$.
  • PL (Positive Large): Center $c = +10 ext{ N}$, width $a = 3.0 ext{ N}$, slope $b = 2$.
  • NL (Negativ Groß): Zentrum $c = -10 ext{ N}$, Breite $a = 3.0 ext{ N}$, Steigung $b = 2$.
  • NM (Negativ Mittel): Zentrum $c = -7 ext{ N}$, Breite $a = 2.5 ext{ N}$, Steigung $b = 2$.
  • PM (Positiv Mittel): Zentrum $c = +7 ext{ N}$, Breite $a = 2.5 ext{ N}$, Steigung $b = 2$.
  • PL (Positiv Groß): Zentrum $c = +10 ext{ N}$, Breite $a = 3.0 ext{ N}$, Steigung $b = 2$.

4.4 Automated Source Script to Generate & Export FIS (`cartpole_16rules.fis`) 4.4 Automatischer Quellcode zur FIS-Generierung & Speicherung (`cartpole_16rules.fis`)

MATLAB Live Script / Script (.m) 
%% ============================================================
%%  FUZZY LOGIC CONTROLLER - CART-PENDULUM (HUST)
%%  Single Unified Mamdani FIS | 4 Inputs x 2 MFs = 16 Rules
%% ============================================================
clc; clear; close all;

% Initialize Mamdani FIS structure
fis = mamfis('NumInputs', 4, 'NumInputMFs', 2, ...
             'NumOutputs', 1, 'NumOutputMFs', 4, ...
             'AddRule', 'none');

% 1. Setup 4 State Inputs
fis.Inputs(1).Name = 'Theta';     fis.Inputs(1).Range = [-pi, pi];
fis.Inputs(1).MembershipFunctions(1).Name = 'N'; fis.Inputs(1).MembershipFunctions(1).Type = 'zmf'; fis.Inputs(1).MembershipFunctions(1).Parameters = [-0.20, 0.20];
fis.Inputs(1).MembershipFunctions(2).Name = 'P'; fis.Inputs(1).MembershipFunctions(2).Type = 'smf'; fis.Inputs(1).MembershipFunctions(2).Parameters = [-0.20, 0.20];

fis.Inputs(2).Name = 'Theta_dot'; fis.Inputs(2).Range = [-15, 15];
fis.Inputs(2).MembershipFunctions(1).Name = 'N'; fis.Inputs(2).MembershipFunctions(1).Type = 'zmf'; fis.Inputs(2).MembershipFunctions(1).Parameters = [-3.50, 3.50];
fis.Inputs(2).MembershipFunctions(2).Name = 'P'; fis.Inputs(2).MembershipFunctions(2).Type = 'smf'; fis.Inputs(2).MembershipFunctions(2).Parameters = [-3.50, 3.50];

fis.Inputs(3).Name = 'X';         fis.Inputs(3).Range = [-0.5, 0.5];
fis.Inputs(3).MembershipFunctions(1).Name = 'N'; fis.Inputs(3).MembershipFunctions(1).Type = 'zmf'; fis.Inputs(3).MembershipFunctions(1).Parameters = [-0.25, 0.25];
fis.Inputs(3).MembershipFunctions(2).Name = 'P'; fis.Inputs(3).MembershipFunctions(2).Type = 'smf'; fis.Inputs(3).MembershipFunctions(2).Parameters = [-0.25, 0.25];

fis.Inputs(4).Name = 'X_dot';     fis.Inputs(4).Range = [-2, 2];
fis.Inputs(4).MembershipFunctions(1).Name = 'N'; fis.Inputs(4).MembershipFunctions(1).Type = 'zmf'; fis.Inputs(4).MembershipFunctions(1).Parameters = [-0.50, 0.50];
fis.Inputs(4).MembershipFunctions(2).Name = 'P'; fis.Inputs(4).MembershipFunctions(2).Type = 'smf'; fis.Inputs(4).MembershipFunctions(2).Parameters = [-0.50, 0.50];

% 2. Setup Output Variable: Force
fis.Outputs(1).Name = 'Force';    fis.Outputs(1).Range = [-12, 12];
fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(1).Name = 'NL'; fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(1).Type = 'gbellmf'; fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(1).Parameters = [3.0, 2, -10];
fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(2).Name = 'NM'; fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(2).Type = 'gbellmf'; fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(2).Parameters = [2.5, 2, -7];
fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(3).Name = 'PM'; fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(3).Type = 'gbellmf'; fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(3).Parameters = [2.5, 2,  7];
fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(4).Name = 'PL'; fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(4).Type = 'gbellmf'; fis.Outputs(1).MembershipFunctions(4).Parameters = [3.0, 2,  10];

% 3. Define 16 Mamdani Heuristic Rules
rules = [
    "If Theta is N and Theta_dot is N and X is N and X_dot is N then Force is NL"
    "If Theta is N and Theta_dot is N and X is N and X_dot is P then Force is NL"
    "If Theta is N and Theta_dot is N and X is P and X_dot is N then Force is NL"
    "If Theta is N and Theta_dot is N and X is P and X_dot is P then Force is NM"
    "If Theta is N and Theta_dot is P and X is N and X_dot is N then Force is NM"
    "If Theta is N and Theta_dot is P and X is N and X_dot is P then Force is NM"
    "If Theta is N and Theta_dot is P and X is P and X_dot is N then Force is NM"
    "If Theta is N and Theta_dot is P and X is P and X_dot is P then Force is NM"
    "If Theta is P and Theta_dot is N and X is N and X_dot is N then Force is PM"
    "If Theta is P and Theta_dot is N and X is N and X_dot is P then Force is PM"
    "If Theta is P and Theta_dot is N and X is P and X_dot is N then Force is PM"
    "If Theta is P and Theta_dot is N and X is P and X_dot is P then Force is PM"
    "If Theta is P and Theta_dot is P and X is N and X_dot is N then Force is PL"
    "If Theta is P and Theta_dot is P and X is N and X_dot is P then Force is PL"
    "If Theta is P and Theta_dot is P and X is P and X_dot is N then Force is PL"
    "If Theta is P and Theta_dot is P and X is P and X_dot is P then Force is PL"
];

fis = addRule(fis, rules);

% Export directly to .fis file for Simulink
writeFIS(fis, 'cartpole_16rules.fis');
disp('=== FIS BUILT & SAVED: cartpole_16rules.fis READY! ===');

Detailed Interconnection Components: Detaillierte Komponentenbeschreibung:

  1. MATLAB Function "Norm Theta":
    Raw tilt angle <angle> passes through a concise normalization function:
    MATLAB Function: Norm Theta 
    function theta_norm = fcn(theta)
% Normalize angle into principal interval [-pi, +pi] to prevent phase winding
theta_norm = atan2(sin(theta), cos(theta));
    Guarantees the angle strictly stays within $[-\pi, +\pi]\ ext{rad}$, preventing integer winding issues during multi-turn swings.
  2. Multiplexer (Mux Block):
    Bundles the 4 state signals in exact FIS order: Port 1: theta, Port 2: theta_dot (W), Port 3: pos, Port 4: vel.

PART 7 Troubleshooting Checklist & Practical Tips for Beginners TEIL 7 Fehlersuche & Praxistipps für Einsteiger

Compiled specifically for students and engineers starting with Simscape Multibody and Fuzzy Control, here are the most frequent pitfalls and solutions:

Zusammengestellt für Studierende und Ingenieure beim Einstieg in Simscape Multibody und Fuzzy-Regelung: Häufigste Fehler und Lösungen:

❌
Issue 1: "Undefined variable smiData" when clicking Run
Root Cause: Simulink was opened without executing the parameter DataFile into the base workspace.
Fix: Run run('full_pendulum_assem_DataFile3.m') in the Command Window, or assign it in Model Properties $ ightarrow$ Callbacks $ ightarrow$ InitFcn for automatic execution.
Problem 1: "Undefined variable smiData" beim Starten
Ursache: Das Modell wurde gestartet, bevor die physikalischen Parameter in den MATLAB-Workspace geladen wurden.
Lösung: Befehl run('full_pendulum_assem_DataFile3.m') im Command Window ausführen oder unter Model Properties $ ightarrow$ Callbacks $ ightarrow$ InitFcn eintragen.
❌
Issue 2: Pendulum flips violently; force acts in the wrong direction
Root Cause: Scrambled input order into the Mux block, or reversed axis convention on the Revolute Joint.
Fix: Verify the 4-channel Mux ordering: [1: Theta, 2: Theta_dot, 3: Pos, 4: Vel], and check the +pi sum block in pole_1_RIGID.
Problem 2: Pendel kippt sofort um; Stellkraft wirkt in die falsche Richtung
Ursache: Vertauschte Eingangsleitungen am Mux oder falsches Vorzeichen am Revolute Joint.
Lösung: Mux-Reihenfolge prüfen: [1: Theta, 2: Theta_dot, 3: Pos, 4: Vel] sowie den +pi-Block in pole_1_RIGID kontrollieren.
✅
Pro Tip: Recommended Solver Configuration:
Multibody mechanical systems exhibit severe stiffness. Use a Variable-step solver with ode23t (mod.stiff/Trapezoidal) or ode15s, configuring Max step size to 0.001 for optimal numerical stability.
Praxistipp: Empfohlene Solver-Auswahl:
Mehrkörpersysteme weisen hohe mathematische Steifigkeit auf. Verwenden Sie einen Variable-step-Solver mit ode23t (mod.stiff/Trapezoidal) oder ode15s bei einer maximalen Schrittweite von 0.001.
